FAQ

About treatment

Not sure what you need?

Will the treatment hurt?

Most treatments are done under local anaesthesia, so you'll feel pressure but not pain. Laser options make many gum treatments even gentler.

Are the listed prices final?

They're starting prices. After your consultation you get a written estimate, and nothing is added without your approval.

How long do implants and aligners take?

Implants usually take about three months from placement to final crown. Aligners typically run 6–12 months depending on your teeth.

Do you offer EMI or accept insurance?

Yes. No-cost EMI is available on treatments above ₹10,000, and we file insurance claims on your behalf.
