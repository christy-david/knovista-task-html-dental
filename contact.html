Before you visit

Good to know

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead means little or no waiting. Emergencies are always seen the same day.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. New patients get a free specialist consultation and a digital X-ray if needed.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Any previous dental X-rays or reports, a list of medicines you take, and your insurance card if you have one.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Reply to your confirmation message on WhatsApp, or call us, ideally a few hours in advance.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), all major cards, and no-cost EMI on treatments above ₹10,000.
