FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find yours? Call us on +91 98470 12345. A dentist answers our helpline.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. Your first consultation with a specialist and a digital X-ray (if needed) are free for new patients.

Do you treat children and the whole family?

Absolutely. Our paediatric dentist sees children from their first tooth, and our Family Care+ plan covers up to four people.

Are you open on weekends?

We're open seven days: 9 AM – 8:30 PM Monday to Saturday, and 10 AM – 2 PM on Sundays.

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead means little or no waiting. Dental emergencies are always seen the same day.

Who will be my treating doctor?

A specialist in the relevant field: an endodontist for root canals, an orthodontist for braces, and so on. You'll meet them at your consultation.

Do you offer EMI or accept insurance?

Yes. No-cost EMI is available on treatments above ₹10,000, and we file insurance claims on your behalf.
